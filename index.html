Our mission

A small studio with a simple rule.

We make smart, joyful, and delightfully silly games for kids — with no ads, ever.

As parents, we believe kids learn best through play. But we saw how advertising interrupted their learning and imagination. Kids deserve games that respect their focus and curiosity.
